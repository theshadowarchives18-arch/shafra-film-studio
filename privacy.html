Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · Application: Shafra Film Studio (@ShafraFilm)

1. Overview

Shafra Film Studio respects your privacy and handles creator data strictly to enable authorized video publishing to TikTok via official TikTok APIs. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to any third parties.

2. Data We Access via TikTok OAuth 2.0

Only after your explicit consent through TikTok Login Kit, Shafra Film Studio accesses the following minimal scopes:

  • user.info.basic: Basic profile identifier (open_id), display name, and avatar URL to verify and display the connected creator account inside the studio interface.
  • video.upload & video.publish: Permission to query creator publishing limits (creator_info/query) and upload or publish videos that you explicitly approve and configure.

3. How We Use and Store Data

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored solely in encrypted private storage for the authorized creator workflow and are never exposed in public repositories or logs.
  • Video files selected for upload are transmitted directly to TikTok's official Content Posting API upload servers and are not shared with external advertising networks.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

OAuth tokens are retained only while your TikTok account remains connected to Shafra Film Studio. You may revoke access at any time via TikTok Settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions), which immediately invalidates all stored tokens. To request full deletion of any stored creator session metadata, contact the Shafra Film editorial team via https://www.youtube.com/@ShafraFilm.

5. Security

All API communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption, PKCE (S256) OAuth 2.0 authorization, and CSRF state validation.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect platform compliance updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
